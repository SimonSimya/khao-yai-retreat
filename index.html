For the kids

Sixty thousand square metres of yes.

The Wild Khao Yai is a fenced resort built around things to do. Nobody will ask for a screen.

Go-kartsBicyclesPaddleboardsSurf skatesArcheryPlaygroundTelescopeCampfireBoard games

Every activity is free of charge for guests.

For the grown-ups

Fire, food and time to talk.

Once the kids are worn out, the evening is yours.

  • Campfire in the evenings. Bring marshmallows.
  • BBQ dinner at your tent. There is a dining area in front of every unit.
  • Breakfast at the Bears Tavern. Coffee first, plans later.
  • A proper gym, open 7:00 to 22:00, plus in-room massage on request.
  • Wifi across the site, for the one call you could not move.
Where you sleep

Camping, minus everything you hate about camping.

Real beds, air conditioning, hot rain showers. The idea is to book side by side so the kids can run between tents.

Glamping tents

About 20 square metres, two queen beds, air conditioning, fridge and a private bathroom.

Caravans

Two queen beds, own bathroom, outdoor dining table. The one the kids will lobby for.

Four-bedroom cottage

Four bedrooms, four bathrooms, a dining table for twelve. For anyone who would rather have walls.

Roughly ฿2,200 to 4,500 per night Depending on the unit and the night. We confirm exact prices when we book.
